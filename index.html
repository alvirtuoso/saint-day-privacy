Saint Day · iOS and Android

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026. Last updated: November 25, 2026.

Saint Day does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. The app works fully offline. Your reminder settings stay on your own device, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy covers the Saint Day mobile application for iPhone, iPad, and Android (the “app”). It describes what the app does with information when you use it. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

None. Saint Day has no accounts, no sign-in, no contact forms, and no advertising. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and the app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or other third-party tracking software.

The app also does not include an embedded web browser, so it cannot observe what you do on the web.

3. Information stored on your device

The app keeps a small amount of information locally so it can remember your choices. This information never leaves your device:

  • Whether daily reminders are turned on or off.
  • The reminder time you selected (8:00 a.m. by default).
  • The dates of the local notifications currently queued for delivery, and the app’s own display settings.

This data is held in the app’s private storage on your device. We have no server, no database, and no way to read it.

4. Notifications

Daily reminders are optional and off by default. If you turn them on, your device asks for notification permission and the app schedules reminders locally on the device. Notifications are generated by your device, not sent from a server, and no information about them is reported to us. You can turn reminders off in the app at any time, or revoke notification permission in your device settings.

5. Links to external websites

When you tap a celebration in the app, Saint Day asks your device’s default web browser to open a search for that saint. This hand-off happens only when you tap, and the app passes only the saint’s name.

From that point the page is served by your browser and the search provider, not by Saint Day. Those services may receive your search query, your IP address, and standard technical information, and they handle it under their own privacy policies. We do not receive, store, or have access to that information.

6. How your information is shared

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any information, because we do not collect any. The app contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks, and no data brokers.

7. Retention and deletion

Because nothing is transmitted off your device, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Your reminder settings remain on your device until you change them, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling Saint Day removes its local settings and cancels its scheduled reminders.

8. Your privacy rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA/CPRA give people rights to access, correct, delete, or port their personal data, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. Saint Day holds no personal data about you, so there is no data for us to disclose, correct, delete, port, or sell. If you believe otherwise, contact us using the details below and we will respond.

9. Children’s privacy

Saint Day is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). No personal information is requested or gathered from any user.

10. Security

The best protection for data that is never transmitted is that it cannot be intercepted. Since Saint Day does not send information over a network and keeps its settings inside the app’s private device storage, there is no server-side store to breach.

11. International users

The app can be used anywhere. Because no data is transferred to us, your use of the app does not involve a cross-border transfer of personal data to us.

12. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds a feature that changes how information is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change. Continued use of the app after an update means the revised policy applies.
